# i18n audit scope

Everything below was read out of this repository on the audit branch
`feature/full-site-i18n-v1`. Nothing here is carried over from memory, from a
previous report, or from the audit brief. Where an existing artefact disagrees
with what the code says, the disagreement is recorded rather than reconciled
silently.

---

## 1. Locales

**Defined in** `src/i18n/config.ts`.

`localeRegistry` (line 45) lists sixteen locales. `LAUNCH_LOCALES` (line 210) names
the seven this release builds:

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `pt-BR` | `/pt-br` | `review` | `machine-drafted` | `,` | `.` |

The remaining nine (`pl`, `it`, `vi`, `th`, `ja`, `ko`, `ar`, `zh-Hans`,
`zh-Hant`) are `status: "planned"` with no dictionary directory and are out of
scope. **`ar` is the only RTL locale in the registry and it is not shipped**, so
no shipped locale is RTL and the RTL checks in §6 of the brief do not apply.
`direction` is nevertheless emitted per locale and is asserted.

`reviewerName` and `reviewedAt` are `null` for all six. **No locale claims a
native review, and this audit does not create one.**

## 2. Routing

Two top-level route groups give English and the localized tree separate root
layouts:

```
src/app/(en)/…              →  /devex-rates/
src/app/(intl)/[locale]/…   →  /de/devex-rates/
```

- English is **bare**, never `/en/`. `src/i18n/locale-path.ts` documents this as
  deliberate and its helpers cannot produce `/en/…`.
- `src/app/(intl)/[locale]/layout.tsx:34` sets `dynamicParams = false`, and
  `generateStaticParams` (line 36) enumerates `renderableLocales()` filtered to
  non-empty prefixes. An unlisted segment is a real 404.
- The URL segment and the BCP 47 tag differ for one locale: tag `pt-BR`,
  segment `/pt-br`. `localeSegment` / `parseLocaleSegment` in
  `src/i18n/locale-path.ts` convert between them; prefixes are matched
  longest-first.
- **Visibility gate:** `src/i18n/visibility.ts`. `isRenderable` returns true for
  a `review` locale only when `ENABLE_REVIEW_LOCALES=true`. `publicLocales()` /
  `isPubliclyVisible` are separate and read `status` alone, so the flag governs
  rendering and never indexing.

## 4. Interpolation, plurals, rich text

- **Syntax is `{name}`**, filled by `src/i18n/interpolate.ts`. Not ICU
  MessageFormat: there is no `plural`, `select`, `selectordinal`, `#`, or
  offset support, and no `%s`. 167 distinct tokens are in use across the
  English catalog.
- **An unknown token is left visible**, deliberately — `interpolate.ts` documents
  a visible `{amount}` as a bug report and a silently-dropped one as a wrong
  figure.
- **Rich text is `src/i18n/rich.tsx`**, which replaces a `{token}` with a
  ReactNode. There are **no HTML tags inside catalog values**; `DictionaryValue`
  in `src/i18n/types.ts` forbids markup so a translation cannot inject any. The
  brief's "rich-text tags balanced" check therefore reduces to token integrity.
- **Plurals are explicit sibling keys `one` / `other`** — 12 groups in the
  English catalog — and the **category is chosen by the call site with
  `count === 1`** (e.g. `src/lib/platform/history.ts:769`,
  `src/features/devex/planner.tsx:529`, `src/views/sources.tsx:78`).

> **Confirmed defect, recorded here and audited in §B.** `count === 1` is not
> the CLDR rule for two shipped locales. Measured with `Intl.PluralRules` in
> this repo's Node:
>
> | Locale | Categories | `select(0)` |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | en | one, other | other |
> | pt-BR | one, many, other | **one** |
> | es | one, many, other | other |
> | id | other | other |
> | fr | one, many, other | **one** |
> | de | one, other | other |
> | tr | one, other | other |
>
> For `fr` and `pt-BR`, zero takes the singular. A hand-written `count === 1`
> renders the plural form for zero in both. `many` in es/fr/pt applies only to
> compact notation, which this site does not use for plural selection, so
> `one`/`other` remains sufficient once selection is correct.

## 5. Numbers: injected vs hardcoded

Canonical source is `src/data/rates.json`, validated at module load by
`src/lib/calculations/rate-registry.ts` (a malformed or unsourced rate fails
`next build`).

**Canonical figures, read this run:**

| Fact | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Standard rate | `0.0038` USD per Earned Robux (`3.80` per 1,000) — status `active` |
| Eligible U.S. 18+ rate | `0.0054` (`5.40` per 1,000) — status `conditional` |
| Legacy rate | `0.0035` (`3.50` per 1,000) — status `legacy` |
| Minimum | `30000` eligible Earned Robux |
| Minimum age | `13` |
| Review cadence | 30 days; critical after 90 |
| Marketplace shares | in-experience 70/30 · marketplace avatar item 30/70 · avatar item in experience 30/30 with 40 to the experience owner |

Figures reach the page two ways:

- **Injected** — `{rate}`, `{minimumEarnedRobux}`, `{amount}` and similar, filled
  from the registry at render. Safe: one source, all locales agree by
  construction.
- **Hardcoded inside a translated string** — the danger zone the audit
  quantifies. Every occurrence is extracted and compared against the registry
  per locale, and flagged as a maintainability risk even when the value is
  currently right.

## 7. Prior reports are treated as unverified

`dist/reports/i18n/coverage-<locale>.json` is written by
`validate-dictionaries.ts` and reports 100% coverage for all six locales. That
check compares **keys and tokens**, not meaning, and cannot see a reversed
negation, a softened obligation or a corrupted decimal. Its passing is not
evidence for anything in §3–§5 of the audit brief, and this audit re-derives
every count independently.

## 8. Scope boundaries

- No English source string is edited to make a translation look correct. Where
  English itself is ambiguous it is reported, not changed.
- No deployment. Deployment readiness is assessed and reported.
- No file is deleted; the diff stays inside i18n.
- Where a language's nuance is beyond what can be verified from this
  repository, the finding is marked `NEEDS-NATIVE-REVIEW` rather than passed.
  **No locale is marked native-reviewed by this audit, because no native
  speaker has read it.**
